Famous Recipes in India

Chicken Biryani

Ingredients: Chicken, basmati rice, onions, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, yogurt, biryani masala, saffron, ghee, mint leaves.

Instructions:

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger, garlic, biryani masala, and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Cook basmati rice until it is 70% done.
  3. In a large pot, heat ghee and fry onions until golden brown. Add the marinated chicken and cook for 10 minutes.
  4. Add the rice on top of the chicken, drizzle saffron water and mint leaves, and cover tightly. Cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with raita and salad.

Veg Manchurian

Ingredients: Mixed vegetables (carrot, cabbage, beans), cornflour, all-purpose flour, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, green chili, vinegar, spring onions.

Instructions:

  1. Grate the mixed vegetables and squeeze out excess water. Combine with cornflour and all-purpose flour to form small balls.
  2. Fry the balls in hot oil until golden brown and set them aside.
  3. In another pan, heat oil, sauté ginger, garlic, and green chilies. Add soy sauce, vinegar, and a little water to make the sauce.
  4. Add the fried veg balls into the sauce and toss them well to coat.
  5. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot with fried rice or noodles.

Chilli Paneer

Ingredients: Paneer, onions, bell peppers, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, cornflour, chili sauce, vinegar, spring onions.

Instructions:

  1. Cut paneer into cubes and lightly fry until golden brown.
  2. In a pan, sauté onions, bell peppers, garlic, and ginger until they are tender.
  3. In a bowl, mix cornflour with water to make a slurry. Add soy sauce, chili sauce, and vinegar to it.
  4. Pour the sauce mixture into the pan and cook until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add the fried paneer to the sauce, toss gently to coat. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot with rice or as a starter.
